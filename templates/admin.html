Registration

Invite links create active accounts in both modes. With requests open, anyone can register a handle and a passkey at /signup, and the account waits for your approval. Past the cap below, new requests are refused until you approve, delete or let expire the ones waiting.

Limits

Quotas apply to member accounts, never to yours. A member over a lowered quota keeps what it has and cannot add more. Rate limits and storage limits apply from the next request.

Unused boards

A member board nobody opens or changes for the days below is deleted with everything on it, and a member left without a board is deleted too. Your boards are never deleted. In its last days a board warns its owner, and opening it keeps it. Deleted boards stay in backups until those age out.
